- **Mage** → *Lethal Ignition* (burst) yoki *Magic Worship* (DoT)
- **Marksman** → *Weapon Master* (hybrid) yoki *Electro Flash* (mobility)
- **Support** → *Focusing Mark* (utility), *Pull Yourself Together* (heal cooldown)
